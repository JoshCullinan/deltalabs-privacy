# DeltaLab Support

**Last updated: 5 September 2026**

DeltaLab is a native app for South African doctors that lets you search and view NHLS TrakCare lab results directly on your phone, using your own NHLS portal login.

## Get help

For any question, problem, or feedback, email **joshcull1@gmail.com**. Describe what you were doing and what happened — a screenshot helps, but please don't include patient names, hospital numbers, or medical-record numbers in anything you send us.

## Frequently asked questions

### I keep getting signed out, or asked to log in again

NHLS portal sessions expire after a period of inactivity, the same as they would in a web browser. If DeltaLab asks you to sign in again, that's expected — just enter your NHLS credentials again. If it happens repeatedly right after signing in, check that your NHLS password hasn't recently changed and that your connection is stable.

### The app says my account is locked, or I can't sign in

NHLS locks the portal after a number of failed login attempts. DeltaLab tries to recover from this automatically the next time you sign in. If it doesn't clear, wait a few minutes before trying again, or use Settings → "Reset NHLS password" to set a new password via NHLS directly.

### An episode shows "Not done by lab" for everything, but I know results exist

This can happen right after your session refreshes in the background. Pull down to refresh the episode, or go back to the episode list and reopen it — DeltaLab will re-fetch fresh results from NHLS. If it persists after a refresh, email us with the episode number (no patient name needed) so we can look into it.

### DeltaLab says "NHLS appears to be down"

This means the app couldn't reach the NHLS portal, or got an error back from it. It's usually genuine — NHLS is temporarily unavailable, or your connection is poor — but on a slow connection, opening a large PDF report can occasionally time out and trigger the same message. Try a normal search again; if that also fails, the portal itself is the likely cause rather than the app.

### Subscription and billing questions

DeltaLab's subscription is sold and billed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID — we never see your payment details. To view, change, or cancel your subscription: **Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions**, or from within DeltaLab: **Settings → Manage subscription**. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I have a privacy question, or want to delete my data

See our [Privacy Policy](../) for exactly what DeltaLab does and doesn't collect, and how to clear on-device data yourself (Recent tab → "Clear all", or Settings → "Clear all data & Logout"). For anything not covered there, email us.

### I found a bug, or have a feature request

We'd like to hear about it. Email **joshcull1@gmail.com** with as much detail as you can — what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead.
