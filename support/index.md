# DeltaLab Support

**Last updated: 10 September 2026**

DeltaLab is a native app for South African doctors that lets you search and view NHLS TrakCare lab results directly on your phone, using your own NHLS portal login. It is free for five patients a day, with an optional subscription for unlimited use.

## Get help

For any question, problem, or feedback, email **joshcull1@gmail.com**. Describe what you were doing and what happened — a screenshot helps, but please don't include patient names, hospital numbers, or medical-record numbers in anything you send us.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does DeltaLab cost?

DeltaLab is **free for five patients a day**. That resets every day at midnight, so you can keep using it for free indefinitely.

If you need more than five in a day, an optional subscription removes the limit entirely. Monthly and annual plans are available, and first-time subscribers get a one-month free trial. You can see the plans in the app under **Settings → See subscription plans**, or when you reach the daily limit.

### What counts towards my five free patients?

**Opening a patient** counts, once per patient per day. Searching does not, so you can search and refine as much as you like for free. Reopening a patient you have already opened today is free and unlimited, so going back to check on the same patient through the day never costs you anything.

The count is kept on your phone and is never sent to us — see the [Privacy Policy](../) for exactly what it stores (a one-way fingerprint that cannot be turned back into a medical-record number), and note that it deliberately survives reinstalling the app, so reinstalling will not give you a fresh five.

### I have reached my daily limit — what can I still do?

Quite a lot:

- **Patients you have already opened today** work exactly as normal.
- **Patients saved on your phone** (the Recent tab, and anyone on your ward round) still open, showing the results already saved on the device. You will see a "Saved results only" note at the top, and anything that would need to fetch fresh data from NHLS — refreshing, opening a new episode's results, the timeline, report PDFs, the handover sheet — will offer you the subscription instead.
- **Searching** still works.

Your allowance resets at midnight. If you need it now, subscribing removes the limit immediately.

### Why can I see the saved results but not refresh them?

Because the results are already on your phone, so there is no reason to hide them from you. What the subscription pays for is fetching **new** data from NHLS. Once your daily allowance is spent, the app will show you everything it already has and stop making new requests until tomorrow.

### DeltaLab keeps asking me to sign in again

NHLS portal sessions expire after a period of inactivity, the same as they would in a web browser. DeltaLab normally signs you back in automatically in the background, using the login saved on your phone, so you shouldn't see a sign-in screen for this.

If it does ask for your password, NHLS has rejected the saved one — usually because your NHLS password was changed or has expired. Sign in with your current password (Settings → Credentials, or the sign-in screen), or use "Forgot password?" to set a new one through NHLS.

### The app says my NHLS account is locked

NHLS locks an account after a few failed sign-in attempts. The lock is applied and lifted by NHLS, not by DeltaLab — we have no way to unlock it for you. Contact NHLS (your NHLS system administrator) directly to have the account unlocked. To help you avoid a lockout, DeltaLab tells you how many failed attempts you have made, so check your username and password carefully before trying again.

If you have forgotten your password, use "Forgot password?" on the sign-in screen (or Settings → "Reset NHLS password"). It opens NHLS's own reset form in your browser, and NHLS emails you a temporary password.

### Results look incomplete, or don't match the lab report

Go back to the patient's episode list, pull down to refresh it, and reopen the episode — DeltaLab will fetch the results from NHLS again. The official lab report PDF (the PDF button on the episode) is always the authoritative record, and DeltaLab is not a diagnostic device, so if the two ever disagree, go by the report.

If it keeps happening, email us describing what you saw. You don't need to include any patient details — please don't send names, hospital numbers or record numbers.

### DeltaLab says "NHLS appears to be down"

This means your phone has an internet connection but the NHLS portal didn't respond, or returned an error. Usually that is genuine — NHLS is temporarily unavailable — and it clears on its own; the app keeps retrying in the background.

Older versions could show this message on a weak or patchy connection even when NHLS was working. Since the September 2026 update the app checks NHLS directly before announcing an outage, and a slow link is reported as "Slow connection to NHLS" instead (below). If you still see "NHLS appears to be down" and a search works when you tap Retry, please tell us — that is the case we want to hear about.

If the app says **"Slow connection to NHLS"**, requests are taking too long to come back. That can be a weak signal or NHLS running slowly — the app can't tell which — so try again in a moment, or move somewhere with better signal. If your phone has no connection at all, it says "You appear to be offline" instead.

### Subscription and billing questions

DeltaLab's subscription is sold and billed entirely by Apple through your Apple ID — we never see your payment details. To view, change, or cancel your subscription: **Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions**, or from within DeltaLab: **Settings → Manage subscription**. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — request one at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

A subscription auto-renews until you cancel it. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge; you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for. If you were on the one-month free trial, cancelling before it ends means you are never charged.

### I subscribed, but the app still shows the free plan

Tap **Restore purchases** — in the app under **Settings → Restore purchases**, or on the subscription screen. That re-checks your Apple ID and should restore access immediately. This is also what to use on a new phone, or after reinstalling. If it still does not work, email us with the Apple ID email you subscribed with (not the password) and we will look into it.

### I have a privacy question, or want to delete my data

See our [Privacy Policy](../) for exactly what DeltaLab does and doesn't collect, and how to clear on-device data yourself (Recent tab → "Clear all", or Settings → "Clear all data & log out"). If you are deleting the app, use "Clear all data & log out" first: iOS keeps some app data in the Keychain after an app is deleted, and that button is what removes it. For anything not covered there, email us.

### I found a bug, or have a feature request
